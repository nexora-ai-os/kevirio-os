# KEVIRIO V3 Strategy Engine

## Purpose

The Strategy Engine turns evidence and Business DNA into versioned strategic alternatives and a concise Owner Decision. It does not autonomously choose material direction.

## Cycle

Observe → frame problem/opportunity → validate evidence quality → generate alternatives → model scenarios → challenge assumptions/risks → recommend with confidence → Owner decide → translate into objectives/operations/experiments → measure → learn → supersede.

## Strategy record

Each version includes Business/Engine scope, objective, diagnosis, alternatives, chosen/non-chosen rationale, assumptions, evidence, truth classes, horizon, resources, expected impact, downside, leading/lagging indicators, stop/scale thresholds, dependencies, reversibility, Owner decision and review date.

## Decision policy

AI may autonomously prioritize reversible internal tasks within approved strategy and budget. Owner approval is mandatory for Business/Engine creation or closure, material capital allocation, Business DNA change, legal/risk acceptance, pricing-policy change, public commitment, external execution enablement and irreversible actions.

## Portfolio strategy

Allocate attention and capital using contribution profit, cash timing, evidence quality, strategic fit, concentration risk and option value. Forecast uncertainty remains visible. Strategies across businesses may share capabilities/assets but cannot cross Workspace data or permission boundaries.

## Experiment link

Uncertain assumptions become hypotheses. Experiment lifecycle is Hypothesis → Design → Approval → Manual or separately gated Execution → Measurement → Learning. Predeclared metrics and stop rules prevent retrospective success claims.

## Failure controls

Stale evidence, circular reasoning, missing alternatives, model monoculture, Goodhart effects and unsupported causality trigger review. Strategy versions expire; no active successor means operations remain within the last approved safe scope or pause.
