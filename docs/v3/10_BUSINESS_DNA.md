# KEVIRIO V3 Business DNA

## Purpose

Business DNA is the immutable-versioned identity and operating philosophy that constrains strategy, AI behavior and presentation. “Immutable” means published versions are never edited; an Owner-approved successor may supersede them.

## DNA schema

| Element | Governs | Required expression |
|---|---|---|
| Mission | present purpose | beneficiaries, value and boundary |
| Vision | desired future | horizon and observable outcome |
| Tone | interpersonal character | preferred and prohibited patterns |
| Brand | identity and promise | positioning, audience, visual/verbal anchors |
| Decision Philosophy | trade-offs | evidence threshold, reversibility, escalation |
| Profit Philosophy | sustainable economics | margin, cash, ethics, reinvestment constraints |
| Quality Standard | definition of acceptable work | measurable criteria and review gates |
| Design Language | visual/system expression | tokens, composition, accessibility |
| Writing Style | Japanese-first communication | voice, terminology, reading level, localization |
| AI Behaviour | workforce conduct | autonomy limits, uncertainty, evidence, failure |
| Owner Authority | reserved decisions | non-delegable and delegable categories |

## Lifecycle and governance

Draft → evidence review → Owner approval → active → superseded/archived. Every strategy, content, employee contract and asset records the DNA version used. Material DNA changes trigger impact analysis and never retroactively relabel earlier outputs.

## Conflict order

Law/safety and explicit Owner decision → Constitution → active Business DNA → approved Strategy → employee/task instructions. AI must surface rather than resolve an unresolved conflict. Missing DNA produces a Locked or Unknown-dependent workflow, never invented brand behavior.

## Multi-business scale

Organization defaults may seed but never silently overwrite Business DNA. Locale variants inherit a declared base and preserve semantic constraints. Comparative learning shares abstract patterns only when permission, provenance and confidentiality allow it.
